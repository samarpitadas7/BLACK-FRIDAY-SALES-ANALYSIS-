# 🛍️ Black Friday Sales Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Black Friday Sales dataset to uncover customer purchasing behavior, demographic trends, and product preferences. The analysis helps identify valuable business insights that can assist retailers in making data-driven marketing and inventory decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics.
- Understand purchasing patterns across different age groups and genders.
- Study the impact of marital status, occupation, and city category on purchases.
- Identify the most popular product categories.
- Generate meaningful visualizations for better business understanding.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains information about customers who purchased products during Black Friday sales.

### Features Include:
- User ID
- Product ID
- Gender
- Age
- Occupation
- City Category
- Stay in Current City Years
- Marital Status
- Product Category
- Purchase Amount

## 📈 Key Insights

- Customer purchasing behavior varies significantly across different age groups.
- Male and female customers show different buying patterns.
- Marital status influences purchasing behavior.
- Certain occupations contribute more to total sales.
- City categories impact customer spending.
- Some product categories generate substantially higher revenue than others.
- Combining demographic attributes provides deeper customer segmentation insights.
